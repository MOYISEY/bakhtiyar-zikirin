# Studio furniture — 2026-10-15

Source: [studio-furniture.blend](../../models/room/studio-furniture.blend), [rebuild instructions](../../models/room/README.md). The source retains 95 bevel modifiers and two packed authored maps; reopening it succeeded. Exported copies merge static parts by material and keep physical drawer nodes separate. Exact hashes and sizes are in [verification](verification-2026-10-15.json).

The default room uses these assets. The previous scene is retained at ?furniture=legacy. The original entrance hinge, collision geometry, scroll route and camera clearance are preserved. The drawer has 0.29 m of bounded travel, pointer/touch dragging and native keyboard activation. The menu remains reachable at 320 × 568. A touch-action rule prevents native page panning from canceling drawer drags.
